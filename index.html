Milestones

Four moments that changed what a car could be.

  1. 1886Karl Benz patents the Motorwagen, widely regarded as the first practical petrol car.
  2. 1908Ford launches the Model T, and the moving assembly line that follows makes cars affordable to ordinary families.
  3. 1997Toyota releases the Prius in Japan, the first mass produced hybrid.
  4. 2008Tesla delivers the Roadster, which proves that an electric car can be fast and desirable.
